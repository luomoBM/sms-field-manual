Santa Magic Sort Field Manual

A compact reference for Santa Magic Sort, the free Christmas liquid sorting game. Everything here was checked against the live site on 2026-11-17.

The rules, compressed

Tap a bottle to pick it up, tap a second bottle, and one dose pours across. No timers, no lives, no move limit. You win when every golden centrepiece vessel holds a single pure colour; working bottles may stay mixed the whole game. Full text on the how-to-play page; the game itself loads in one click.

The thirty-challenge ladder

Five menu pages, six boards each, opening with Evergreen Ember (Ruby), Snow Globe Wish (Ruby), Sugar Pine Spark (Ice Blue), Sleigh Bell Serenade (Ice Blue), Frost Tower Glow (Mint) and Holly Hearth (Mint). Only the first is unlocked on a fresh save. Every name is catalogued on the challenges page.

The method

Rank the golden centrepieces by doses-to-pure and finish the cheapest one completely before starting another. Completed colours shrink the problem. When a board still fights back, the seven-step stuck-board guide ends with the cheapest move in the game: a free restart.

The two companions

Liquid Sort Deluxe is classic water-sort pouring with a countdown and two pressure valves: a reward-ad extra tube and an unlimited-time toggle. Holiday Hex Sort swaps pouring for drag-and-collect: ten matching hex tiles in one stack clear, against a hard timer. Compare all three on the games-like page.

Playing on a school network

The game lives on one official domain and there are no mirrors - the unblocked page says so directly and covers the realistic options. For seasonal variety under the same domain, the holiday collection lists everything.
